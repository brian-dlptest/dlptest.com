**Proofpoint**, the email security and insider threat vendor owned by private equity firm **Thoma Bravo**, is reportedly in talks to acquire **Varonis Systems**, according to a Bloomberg report citing people familiar with the matter. If it happens, it would rank among the largest technology take-privates since this year's software stock selloff.

The report says Proofpoint is negotiating deal terms with Varonis, which has been working with advisers on acquisition interest. Varonis shares jumped roughly 13% on the news, with the company carrying a market value of approximately $4.7 billion before the report. Sources said an announcement could come in the coming weeks, but no final decision has been made. The talks could collapse or another bidder could show up. Proofpoint, Thoma Bravo, and Varonis haven't confirmed anything publicly.

Proofpoint already acquired DSPM pioneer [**Normalyze**](/proofpoint-to-acquire-normalyze-ai-another-dspm-acquisition/) in 2024 to add posture management to its email and insider threat DLP. Adding Varonis, one of the largest independent data security platforms, would give one vendor DLP, DSPM, data access governance, and insider risk monitoring. That puts Proofpoint more directly against **Cyera**, which has been building its own platform through acquisitions (including a recent NHI governance deal with **Oasis Security**), as well as **Securiti**, **Sentra**, and **Concentric AI**.

Remember this is reported deal talk from unnamed insiders, not a signed deal. If you're running Varonis DSPM or DAG along with Proofpoint DLP, or evaluating either one, watch this closely. A completed deal would affect the roadmap, licensing, and integrations for both.
