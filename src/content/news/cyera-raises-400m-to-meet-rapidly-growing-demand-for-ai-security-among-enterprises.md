**Cyera** announced a **$400 million Series F** led by Blackstone. That brings its total funding to over $1.7 billion and triples the company's valuation to **$9 billion** in one year, which makes it one of the top-funded security startups in the world. Investors like Accel, Sequoia Capital, and Redpoint joined Blackstone in the round.

The pitch is **agentic AI**. As enterprises move from simple generative AI to more autonomous agents, Cyera says the risk is growing faster than traditional tools can manage. IDC predicts that by 2030, up to **20% of G1000 organizations** will face disruptions due to poor AI agent governance. Cyera's answer is one AI-native platform that combines Data Security Posture Management (DSPM), Data Loss Prevention (DLP), and identity. It calls that the "cornerstone of enterprise trust."

Here's what Cyera says is behind its recent **3.4x revenue growth**:

- **AI Guardian:** A security platform for AI-driven businesses, with risk detection and automated safeguards for proprietary data.
- **Enterprise customers:** Cyera now secures data for **20% of the Fortune 500**, across financial services, healthcare, retail, and global telecom.
- **Size:** The company has grown to over 1,100 team members in 15 countries across North America, EMEA, and APAC.

The funding also follows partnerships with **Microsoft Purview, AWS, and Cohesity**. Vishal Amin, Senior Managing Director at Blackstone, said Cyera's AI-native approach is essential to eliminating the "blind spots" legacy systems create during major platform shifts.

Cyera says the $400 million goes toward product innovation and global expansion.
