**Cyera**, the data security posture management (DSPM) vendor that recently raised $600M at a $12B valuation (roughly $2.3B raised to date), is reportedly in advanced talks to acquire fellow Israeli firm **Oasis Security** in a deal that could exceed $1B. Globes reported it, and Calcalist/Ctech and Ynetnews back it up. The deal would be a minority in cash (~30%) with the rest in Cyera stock.

Oasis is a non-human identity (NHI) management platform that raised $120M in March 2026. Buying it would take Cyera beyond data discovery and posture and into governing *who and what* can touch that data. That goes straight at the AI agent identity problem, where service accounts, tokens, and autonomous agents far outnumber people and can do a lot of damage.

It follows Cyera's Trail and Genie acquisitions, and it pushes on the line between DSPM and identity, which most enterprises still buy and run separately.

Keep in mind this is a **reported deal that hasn't closed**. The terms, and even if it happens at all, aren't confirmed. It's also an identity acquisition and not a pure DLP or DSPM one.
