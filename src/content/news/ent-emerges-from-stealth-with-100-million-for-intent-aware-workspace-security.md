**Ent** emerged from stealth with **$100 million** in seed financing led by **Decibel**, with participation from **Sequoia**, **Crosspoint Capital Partners**, **Craft Ventures**, **Shield Capital**, **Felicis**, and **In-Q-Tel**. The company is positioning itself as an "intent-aware" workspace security layer that evaluates risky human and AI-agent actions before they become incidents.

The DLP relevance is unusually explicit for an endpoint-security launch. Ent says Global 2000 customers use the platform to detect insider risk, govern AI usage, prevent data loss, stop last-mile threats, and investigate incidents with behavioral context. The product runs as a lightweight agent across Windows, macOS, Linux, and browser-extension deployments, observing behavior across applications, browsers, workflows, data movement, and local runtimes.

### Why DLP Teams Should Watch

Traditional endpoint DLP often sees the file, destination, or user action after a policy has already been evaluated in a narrow channel. Ent's pitch is that security teams need to understand intent at the moment of use: why a user or AI agent is moving data, whether that behavior fits normal work, and whether a just-in-time intervention should stop it.

That overlaps with several budgets buyers often separate: endpoint DLP, insider risk management, AI governance, and EDR-adjacent prevention. If Ent can make those controls operational without recreating the tuning burden of legacy DLP, it will be a meaningful entrant. If it cannot, the category risk is familiar: rich behavioral context that still produces another queue for analysts to triage.

### What To Validate

The launch materials say Ent is already generally available and deployed with Global 2000 customers. Practitioners evaluating it should ask which data movement channels are enforceable today, how customer-defined policy is expressed, whether AI-agent activity is distinguishable from human activity in audit trails, and how interventions integrate with existing EDR, SIEM, SOAR, IAM, and DLP workflows.
