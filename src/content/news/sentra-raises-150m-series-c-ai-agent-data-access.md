**Sentra**, the Israeli DSPM vendor founded by a former Unit 8200 commander, closed a $150 million Series C led by **Capital One Ventures**. The round was corroborated by BankInfoSecurity and Calcalist.

The money is framed around controlling what autonomous AI agents can reach inside corporate data stores, not just the classic cloud discovery and classification work Sentra built its name on. The company says customers increasingly want an end-to-end data security platform rather than isolated tools, with DLP, DSPM, AI security, and identity treated as connected parts of one system.

Sentra also says it's expanding toward AI governance and agentic AI security, meaning knowing which models and agents exist and what data they can touch. That broadens the buyer group past the CISO to CIOs and CDOs. The company still says core DSPM work, classification, data coverage, and tying data context to identity and access, remains essential. This is the same pattern as a lot of DSPM vendors lately: bolt agent-access governance onto discovery as a way into the AI-data-control budget line.
