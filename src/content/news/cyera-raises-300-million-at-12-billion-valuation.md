**Cyera** raised **$300 million** at a reported **$12 billion valuation**, according to Calcalist, with **Evolution Equity Partners** leading the round. The report says Georgian, Greenoaks, Lightspeed, Sequoia, Sapphire, Redpoint, Cyberstarts, Coatue, Accel, and Spark Capital also participated.

That's only five months after Cyera's January round at a **$9 billion** valuation, and it brings total capital raised to more than **$2 billion**. Calcalist and FinSMEs describe the financing as raised. TechCrunch reported the round as being finalized and noted that a Cyera spokesperson disputed some of the numbers. Either way, investors are still paying up for AI data security.

### Why DLP buyers should care

This isn't just a DSPM story anymore. Cyera has used acquisitions, including **Trail Security**, **Ryft**, and **Genie Security**, to push into enforcement, AI security, and endpoint data protection. Buyers are now asking if discovery, classification, DLP, identity context, and AI agent governance should be bought separately or from one platform.

The question I'd ask is how quickly Cyera can turn this money and these acquired teams into product coverage that actually fits together. A bigger balance sheet helps with global sales, support, and M&A, but a DLP program still depends on channel coverage, signal quality, explainability, and enforcement that doesn't get in people's way. You can't just buy those with a higher valuation.
