[Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/w1j2xejw4) reports that **Cyera** raised **$300 million** at a **$12 billion** valuation, led by **Evolution Equity Partners** with participation from **Georgian**, **Greenoaks**, **Lightspeed Venture Partners**, **Sequoia Capital**, **Sapphire Ventures**, **Redpoint Ventures**, **Cyberstarts**, **Coatue**, **Accel**, and **Spark Capital**.

The timing is striking: Cyera announced a **$400 million** round at a **$9 billion** valuation only five months earlier. Calcalist says the new financing brings total capital raised above **$2 billion**, putting Cyera among the most valuable Israeli startups and well ahead of most private security vendors by funding scale.

### From DSPM Wedge to Platform Ambition

Cyera started with the classic **DSPM** buyer problem: discover sensitive data, classify it, understand who can access it, and reduce exposure across cloud environments. The recent spending pattern shows a broader platform thesis. Calcalist notes a rapid acquisition run that includes **Genie Security** for endpoint-oriented data protection, **Ryft** for AI data access and governance, **Trail Security** for DLP, plus earlier purchases of **Otterize** and **Shape AI**.

That matters for DLP teams because "data security platform" is no longer just posture visibility. The leading vendors are racing to connect discovery, identity context, AI-agent governance, endpoint coverage, and enforcement into one operating model.

### What This Means for DLP Buyers

The market signal is that investors still believe a consolidated data-security control plane can command a premium, especially as enterprises expose sensitive data to SaaS, cloud data stores, and generative AI workflows. For practitioners, the diligence question is whether Cyera's acquired capabilities appear as integrated workflows or as adjacent modules that still require separate tuning, policy work, and incident triage.

The financing also raises the competitive bar for smaller DSPM and DLP startups. Buyers should expect more aggressive platform packaging, faster roadmap claims around AI data security, and continued pressure on standalone vendors to prove depth in specific enforcement channels.
