[Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/w1j2xejw4) reports that **Cyera** raised **$300 million** at a **$12 billion valuation**, led by **Evolution Equity Partners** with participation from existing investors including **Georgian**, **Greenoaks**, **Lightspeed Venture Partners**, **Sequoia Capital**, **Sapphire Ventures**, **Redpoint Ventures**, **Cyberstarts**, **Coatue**, **Accel**, and **Spark Capital**.

The reported round would bring Cyera's total capital raised to more than **$2 billion** and comes only months after the company announced a **$400 million Series F** at a **$9 billion** valuation. That pace is notable even in a security market where AI-adjacent vendors are commanding premium multiples.

### Why This Matters for DLP and DSPM Buyers

Cyera is no longer being funded like a narrow **DSPM** tool. The company's recent strategy has been to assemble a broader data security platform spanning discovery, classification, access governance, AI data controls, and enforcement. Recent acquisitions cited in the same reporting include **Ryft** for AI data-access governance and **Genie Security** for endpoint-oriented data protection.

For practitioners, the takeaway is less about valuation headlines and more about platform gravity. Buyers evaluating DLP refreshes, DSPM rollouts, and GenAI data controls should expect Cyera to keep pushing the "single control plane for data security" message against both legacy DLP incumbents and cloud/security platform vendors that have acquired DSPM capabilities.

### Valuation Caveat

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/cyera-eyes-12b-valuation-at-80x-arr-multiple-despite-operating-losses/) reported additional details on the round and said a Cyera spokesperson disputed the numbers cited as inaccurate. As of this writing, I do not see a matching Cyera press release for this June financing, so treat the exact valuation and round mechanics as reported figures rather than company-confirmed terms.
