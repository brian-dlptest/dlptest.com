Proofpoint announced its intent to acquire Normalyze, a Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) pioneer, in a deal set to close in November 2024. It adds Normalyze's AI-powered DSPM to Proofpoint's human-centric security platform, covering SaaS, PaaS, public clouds, and on-premises systems.

Together they want to help organizations discover, classify, and prioritize sensitive data, manage overprivileged access, and get security insights they can act on. Normalyze also brings an AI-driven data scanner and quantified risk assessment tools, which help find high-impact risks and potential data breaches.
