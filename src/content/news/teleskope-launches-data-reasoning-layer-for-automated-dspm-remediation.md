**Teleskope** launched its **Data Reasoning Layer**, positioning it as the decision layer between DSPM-style discovery and the enforcement work that DLP teams actually need completed.

The announcement is not just another dashboard story. Teleskope says the layer classifies sensitive data in business context, decides which action fits the customer's policy, and enforces the result natively with auditability and rollback. The available actions include redaction, quarantine, access revocation, relocation, encryption, and deletion.

### Closing the discovery-to-remediation gap

For practitioners, the interesting claim is the handoff reduction. Classic DSPM can surface risky data, while classic DLP can block defined movements, but many programs still rely on tickets, SOAR workflows, or manual triage to connect the two. Teleskope is trying to make that loop continuous, especially for exposure in SaaS and AI tools.

The release also adds a context-aware classification engine, natural-language AI Search, policy ingestion, and automated remediation across **OpenAI**, **Slack**, and **Claude**. That puts Teleskope in the same broader market conversation as vendors arguing that data security posture, DLP, and GenAI controls are converging into one operating model.
