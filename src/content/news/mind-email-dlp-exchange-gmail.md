A day after its Copilot and Microsoft 365 integration news, **MIND** announced a dedicated Email DLP module covering Microsoft Exchange and Gmail. The pitch is agentless coverage: nothing gets installed on Exchange or Gmail, and setup is automatic.

The company says the module lets security teams block risky outbound email in real time, from any device, using the same classification policies MIND applies across GenAI, agentic AI, SaaS, on-premise file shares and endpoints.

Email DLP is one of the oldest DLP channels, and it has historically been a manual-review burden because of classification accuracy problems. Two MIND announcements in two days, covering Copilot integrations and then email, suggest a coordinated push to fill out channel coverage.
