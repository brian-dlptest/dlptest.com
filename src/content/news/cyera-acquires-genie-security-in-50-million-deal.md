[Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/hke7g7jjfl) reports that **Cyera** acquired **Genie Security** for an estimated **$50 million**. That's a fast exit for a company founded only five months ago with roughly five employees. **Genie** had raised about **$3 million** in seed funding from **Mensch Capital** and **Dynamic Loop**, with angels including **Assaf Rappaport** (Wiz) and executives from **Okta** and **Elastic**.

### What Genie does

**Genie** builds endpoint data protection: agents on endpoints that spot attempts to leak sensitive information in real time, including through human actions and generative AI tools like **Claude**. Calcalist says it was already deployed across hundreds of endpoints in Israel and the United States.

Founders **Nadav Noy** (CEO) and **Noam Dotan** (CTO), both with **Unit 8200** and prior cybersecurity startup backgrounds, will join Cyera's enterprise **DLP** division. The technology is expected to fold into Cyera's platform, which the company says is deployed at a meaningful share of **Fortune 500** customers.

### Cyera's acquisition spree

**Cyera** keeps buying. It was recently valued around **$9 billion**, and prior acquisitions in the same reporting include **Trail Security** (roughly **$162 million**), **Ryft** (estimated **$100 million**), **Otterize**, and **Shape AI**. Headcount is reportedly near **1,500** across **15** countries.

So how does Genie's **endpoint DLP** fit with Cyera's DSPM and cloud discovery? [Trail was pitched as AI-powered DLP](/cyera-is-acquiring-trail-security/). Genie is explicitly about the endpoint and exfiltration, including **GenAI** upload and paste. That's closer to the channel coverage a lot of teams care about when they look at "DSPM plus enforcement."

The next thing to watch for is a formal Cyera press release with product details, especially if Genie's endpoint controls ship as a separate SKU or get merged into existing Cyera DLP modules. Either way, DSPM leaders are buying enforcement and insider risk capabilities and not stopping at discovery.
