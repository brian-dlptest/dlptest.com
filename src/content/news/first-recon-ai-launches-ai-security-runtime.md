**First Recon AI** announced general availability of its AI Security Runtime. It inspects every AI interaction (human to model, agent to tool, and agent to agent) and applies policy inline *before* data reaches a model.

The DLP part is concrete. A Windows and macOS endpoint agent enforces device-level controls across both sanctioned and shadow AI tools, blocking sensitive data from leaving managed endpoints. That makes this GenAI and endpoint DLP, not the fuzzier "AI governance" bucket. The company also ships a browser and desktop "secure AI workspace" as a managed alternative to public chat apps, which will look familiar if you've deployed CASB prompt inspection or browser isolation for LLM traffic.

This is one more example of endpoint DLP coming together with LLM prompt and response inspection. The questions are the usual ones. How does the inline policy engine handle latency and false positives on real prompts? What does the audit trail look like for compliance reviews? How does coverage compare to Microsoft Purview's runtime DLP, the AI controls in Zscaler and Netskope, and the newer autonomous DLP vendors?

This is a product launch, not a funding event. No round or valuation was disclosed. If you're already scoping AI controls, add it to your bake-off.
