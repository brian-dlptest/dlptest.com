**Cyera** has reportedly raised **$300 million** at a **$12 billion valuation**, according to Calcalist, just five months after announcing a $400 million round at a $9 billion valuation.

The latest round was led by **Evolution Equity Partners**, with participation from existing and returning investors including **Georgian**, **Greenoaks**, **Lightspeed Venture Partners**, **Sequoia Capital**, **Sapphire Ventures**, **Redpoint Ventures**, **Cyberstarts**, **Coatue**, **Accel**, and **Spark Capital**. Calcalist reports that the financing brings Cyera's total capital raised to more than $2 billion.

### Why This Matters for Data Security Buyers

Cyera is no longer being valued as a narrow DSPM point product. The company is being priced like a platform vendor for the enterprise data-security layer that sits underneath AI adoption: discovery, classification, access understanding, data loss prevention, and increasingly controls for how human and non-human actors touch sensitive information.

That matters for buyers because consolidation is changing the short list. Large security platforms have been acquiring DSPM-adjacent vendors, while Cyera is using capital and acquisitions to argue it can remain an independent data-security platform. Its recent purchases of Ryft and Genie Security push the roadmap deeper into agentic AI data access and endpoint DLP, not just cloud data posture.

### A Valuation Signal, Not Just a Funding Headline

Practitioners should separate the market signal from the vendor claim. A $12 billion valuation does not prove implementation maturity, policy quality, or incident reduction. It does suggest investors believe the budget line for AI-era data controls is expanding quickly enough to support an independent category leader.

For DLP and DSPM teams, the question is whether that capital turns into better coverage across SaaS, cloud, endpoint, and AI workflows - or simply a broader platform story that still needs proof in production evaluations.
