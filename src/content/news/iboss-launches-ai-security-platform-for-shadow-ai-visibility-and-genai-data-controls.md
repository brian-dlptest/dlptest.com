**iboss**, an SSE/CASB data security vendor, launched a free "AI Security Platform" for the shadow AI problem: people pasting sensitive data into tools nobody sanctioned. The free tier discovers and risk-classifies GenAI usage (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and desktop apps like Cursor) across endpoints, and ties prompt and response history back to individual users.

The actual data loss prevention is in the paid tier. That's where you get AI policy enforcement, leak prevention, and governance of AI agents, all running on the same gateway engine iboss uses for web and email DLP.

What's interesting is the packaging. It's free shadow AI discovery that leads to a GenAI DLP upsell. If you're already looking at email and web DLP or an SSE, free visibility into unsanctioned AI usage is an easy way to measure your exposure before you spend any money. It also shows how existing vendors are adding GenAI controls to the gateways they already have.

Just remember that the free part is *discovery*. The prevention, the part that actually blocks a leaking prompt, is the paid product. Check the iboss release for the exact paid-tier DLP claims before you put it on a shortlist.
