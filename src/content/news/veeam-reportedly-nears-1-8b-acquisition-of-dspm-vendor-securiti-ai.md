Data protection giant **Veeam** is reportedly in advanced talks to acquire **AI security and data security posture management firm Securiti** for **$1.8 billion**, according to Bloomberg. It would be Veeam's largest acquisition to date and would speed up its move from backup and recovery into cybersecurity.

Valuations across data protection have gone up a lot on enterprise spending for **AI and data resilience**. Rivals **Rubrik** and **Commvault** have both seen big gains, and Veeam itself reached a $15B valuation after a $2B funding round in late 2024.

Securiti was founded in 2019 and is led by **Rehan Jalil**. It has top rankings from Forrester and IDC for its **AI-driven data discovery, privacy, and security capabilities**. Securiti AI's revenue was $75.9 million in October 2024, about 23 times its 2024 revenue.

If it goes through, this would expand Veeam's cybersecurity portfolio and help it ahead of a potential **IPO**, though integrating Securiti's large R&D team will be a big job.
