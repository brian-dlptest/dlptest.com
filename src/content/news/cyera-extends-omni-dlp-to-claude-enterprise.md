**Cyera** announced an integration between **Omni DLP** and **Anthropic's Claude Compliance API**, extending Cyera's data-security controls into **Claude Enterprise** conversations, uploaded files, and user activity.

The release is notable because it moves beyond generic "AI governance" language into the DLP workflows practitioners actually have to operate: detecting PII, PHI, financial records, proprietary content, anomalous usage patterns, and potential insider-risk signals inside an AI application that employees may use every day.

### Why This Matters for DLP

Traditional DLP programs usually watch channels: endpoint copy, email, web uploads, SaaS sharing, or cloud storage. Claude-style enterprise AI changes the path. Sensitive data may be surfaced through prompts, file uploads, retrieval workflows, or model responses rather than a classic outbound transfer event.

Cyera is positioning the integration as a way to apply existing DLP and data-security policies to Claude without rebuilding them manually. That matters if security teams want AI usage to fold into existing incident review, audit trails, and remediation workflows instead of becoming another standalone console.

### Buyer Lens

The practical question is whether this becomes a durable enforcement pattern or a point integration tied to one AI vendor's compliance API. For Cyera customers already standardizing on its DSPM and DLP stack, Claude visibility is useful. For teams evaluating broader AI controls, the differentiator will be how consistently Cyera normalizes policy, alert quality, and forensics across Claude, browser-based GenAI, SaaS data stores, and endpoint-adjacent leakage paths.
