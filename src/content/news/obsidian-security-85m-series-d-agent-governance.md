**Obsidian Security** announced an $85 million Series D at a $1.1 billion valuation, led by Crescent Cove Advisors with **Greylock Partners** and **Menlo Ventures** participating. That takes total funding past $200 million and puts Obsidian in a fast-growing category: governing AI agents and non-human identities inside SaaS.

The platform monitors what agents like **Microsoft** Copilot Studio, **Salesforce** Agentforce, **n8n**, and **Anthropic**'s Claude actually do once they have access to enterprise data. That's a different problem than SSPM misconfiguration alerts or DSPM data classification. When agents have write and delete permissions in CRM records, ticketing systems, and file stores, the question goes from "who can see this data" to "what is this autonomous process doing with it, and can I revoke that mid-action."

The timing is interesting. This comes around the same time as **Zenity**'s reported $125 million Series C and **Onyx Security**'s $113 million raise, both also focused on AI agent behavior. That's three big raises in a few weeks, which tells me investors see agent governance as its own category.

If it becomes a separate purchase, expect DSPM vendors to either buy into this space or ship their own agent monitoring in the next few product cycles. Obsidian says the money goes toward platform expansion, and it describes its mission as making AI adoption "unstoppable" by governing identity, access, and data across every third-party app the business runs.
