**Harmonic Security**, a workforce AI security platform, announced a $50 million Series B led by **Crosspoint Capital Partners** with participation from existing investors, bringing the company's total funding to $76 million. The round lands amid a broader wave of AI-governance funding and positions Harmonic as a direct comparable to **MIND** and **Cyberhaven** in the race to control sensitive-data exposure through generative AI tools.

The product addresses a gap that traditional DLP tools often miss: real-time discovery, classification, and control of how employees actually use AI, spanning browser-based AI monitoring, desktop AI applications, and increasingly agentic workflows. Rather than relying solely on static policy rules, Harmonic uses small language models to classify sensitive data in context — letting security teams understand what kind of data is flowing into which AI tools and apply low-friction controls rather than blanket bans.

Scale claims from the announcement are substantial for a company at this funding stage: more than 100 companies are listed as customers, with coverage reportedly extending to millions of employees. That customer base size suggests genuine enterprise traction rather than early pilot deployments, and it reinforces that GenAI-aware data controls have moved from a niche concern to a mainstream line item in data protection budgets.

For insider risk and DSPM practitioners, this is a funding note worth tracking as a market signal rather than a product launch: it confirms investor appetite for GenAI-specific data governance plays is still strong, even as the category gets crowded with well-funded competitors tackling overlapping use cases around shadow AI, prompt-level data leakage, and agentic workflow risk.
