**Above Security**, the AI-native managed insider-threat platform, used Black Hat USA to announce a strategic investment from the CrowdStrike Falcon Fund alongside a native integration with the **CrowdStrike** Falcon platform. This is a separate event from Above's earlier funding round — the news here is the plumbing, not the check.

## Why the integration matters more than the money

Above correlates Next-Gen SIEM endpoint, identity, and third-party telemetry into investigation-ready cases, then streams completed investigations back into Falcon. In practice that makes insider risk a native outcome of the platform rather than a bolt-on. Above's pitch is a fleet of always-on, continuously reasoning AI agents that analyze behavior across identities, applications, data movement, and workflow context.

For DLP and insider-risk buyers, the decision this sharpens is familiar: if you're already standardized on Falcon, do you buy a standalone insider-risk tool, or consume that capability through your endpoint vendor? Data-movement telemetry feeding an EDR/SIEM incumbent nudges that calculus toward consolidation — worth watching if you're mid-cycle on an insider-risk RFP.

## A note on the deal size and prior round

The investment is modest and strategic — CrowdStrike put several million dollars into the Israeli startup, deepening a partnership centered on insider threats. Expect the value to accrue through distribution and integration rather than the headline figure.

One data-quality flag worth carrying forward: the vendor PR references Above's recent $50 million round led by Ballistic Ventures, Merlin Ventures, and Norwest, while Calcalist frames the prior raise as a $43 million Series A led by Merlin Ventures, Ballistic Ventures, and Norwest Venture Partners with participation from Jump Capital. The $50M vs. $43M discrepancy is unresolved between the two sources — cite accordingly rather than silently picking one.
