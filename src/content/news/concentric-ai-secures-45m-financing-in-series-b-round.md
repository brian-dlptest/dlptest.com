Concentric AI, a data security posture management (DSPM) product, raised a $45 million Series B, bringing its total funding to over $67 million. Top Tier Capital Partners and HarbourVest Partners led the round, with CyberFuture and existing investors like Ballistic Ventures participating. Garth Timoll Sr., General Partner of Top Tier Capital, joins Concentric AI's board.

The company says it has seen 300% year-over-year growth and tripled its customer base. Its platform uses natural language processing to discover, classify, monitor, and remediate data risks across on-premise and cloud environments, including generative AI tools like Microsoft Copilot. Concentric AI is going after a data security market projected to exceed $8 billion by 2027.
