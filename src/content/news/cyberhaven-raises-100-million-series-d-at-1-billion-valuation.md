**Cyberhaven** raised **$100 million in Series D funding**, led by **StepStone Group** with participation from **Schroders** and **Industry Ventures**. That brings its total funding to **$250 million** and values the company at **$1 billion**, a **7x increase in one year**. (Disclaimer: I work at Cyberhaven.)

Its claim to fame is **data lineage**. It uses a proprietary **Large Lineage Model (LLiM)** to trace and analyze data movement in real time, which the company says lets customers detect and prevent risks to sensitive data better than traditional security tools.

CEO **Howard Ting** talked about the need for next-gen security tools in the AI and cloud era, when data is fragmented and always moving. The funding goes toward **platform expansion**, **market growth**, and **continued innovation**.
