Bedrock Data, a fast-rising provider in the data security posture management (DSPM) space, has announced a **$25 million Series A funding round** led by Greylock Partners, with participation from Mangusta Capital, Mantis Venture Capital, Pier 88 Investment Partners and others. The investment marks a major milestone as enterprises race to secure data across increasingly complex cloud and AI ecosystems.

## **Why This Matters: Data Security Is Being Rewritten for the AI Era**

As organizations adopt foundation models, agentic AI, and massive-scale cloud architectures, traditional security tools built around perimeters, identities, and infrastructure can no longer keep up. Bedrock Data’s 2025 Enterprise Data Security Confidence Index highlights this gap, revealing that **82% of cybersecurity leaders struggle to find and classify organizational data**.

Bedrock Data’s platform tackles this challenge with its **Metadata Lake**, a continuously updated graph that discovers, classifies, and contextualizes data across on-prem, cloud, SaaS, and AI environments. This enables enterprises to:

- Detect exposure and risk in real time
- Close least-privilege access gaps
- Strengthen DSPM programs
- Support responsible AI with visibility into data use

Greylock partner Jason Risch, who joins Bedrock’s board, emphasized that AI-driven environments require “scale and visibility across the full data surface,” something legacy tools cannot deliver.

## **Expanding AI Governance With ArgusAI**

Alongside the funding, Bedrock Data announced **ArgusAI**, a new capability that provides organizations with transparency into the data accessed by AI models and agents during both training and inference. With this insight, enterprises can evaluate risks, prevent sensitive data leakage, and enforce natural-language security policies consistently across systems.

## **Momentum Across Industries**

Bedrock Data’s platform has seen rapid adoption in sectors including financial services, healthcare, and technology. Its ecosystem now includes leading partners such as **Sysdig, Wiz, and Panther**, which leverage Bedrock’s data context to enhance their own platforms.

The company has also earned industry recognition, including:

- Fortune Top 50 Cybersecurity Company of 2025
- SINET16 Innovator
- Awards from CODiE, InfoWorld, SC Media, Global InfoSec Awards and Intellyx

## **A Platform Built for Scale and Trust**

Bedrock Data’s architecture, powered by a patented Metadata Lake and Serverless Outpost, allows the platform to autonomously discover and classify data **without moving it outside customer boundaries**. Its open, API-first approach integrates with existing tools and enables natural-language policy enforcement and automated remediation at enterprise scale.

As AI redefines how enterprises build and secure systems, Bedrock Data aims to become the **source of truth for data sensitivity, access, and lineage** across the modern data stack.
