**Bedrock Data**, a data security posture management (DSPM) vendor, announced a **$25 million Series A** led by Greylock Partners, with participation from Mangusta Capital, Mantis Venture Capital, Pier 88 Investment Partners, and others.

Bedrock's own 2025 Enterprise Data Security Confidence Index says **82% of cybersecurity leaders struggle to find and classify organizational data**. Its answer is the **Metadata Lake**, a continuously updated graph that discovers, classifies, and adds context to data across on-prem, cloud, SaaS, and AI environments. Bedrock says that lets customers:

- Detect exposure and risk in real time
- Close least-privilege access gaps
- Strengthen DSPM programs
- Support responsible AI with visibility into data use

Greylock partner Jason Risch joins Bedrock's board. He said AI-driven environments require "scale and visibility across the full data surface," and that legacy tools can't deliver it.

### ArgusAI

Bedrock also announced **ArgusAI**, which shows what data AI models and agents access during training and inference. The idea is that you can evaluate the risk, prevent sensitive data leaks, and enforce natural-language security policies across systems.

### Customers and recognition

Bedrock says it's seeing adoption in financial services, healthcare, and technology. Its partners include **Sysdig, Wiz, and Panther**, which use Bedrock's data context in their own platforms.

The company has also picked up some industry recognition:

- Fortune Top 50 Cybersecurity Company of 2025
- SINET16 Innovator
- Awards from CODiE, InfoWorld, SC Media, Global InfoSec Awards and Intellyx

The architecture uses a patented Metadata Lake and Serverless Outpost to discover and classify data **without moving it outside customer boundaries**. It's API-first and supports natural-language policy enforcement and automated remediation. Bedrock says it wants to be the **source of truth for data sensitivity, access, and lineage**.
