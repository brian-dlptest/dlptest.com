Tel Aviv-based **Bay** emerged from stealth on November 10, 2026 with $50 million in combined funding: a $37 million Series A round led by Battery Ventures and a previously undisclosed $13 million seed round led by YL Ventures and Striker Venture Partners. The company was founded by three former Unit 8200 officers.

Bay's product is a control plane that discovers, understands, and controls AI agent actions, closer to runtime policy enforcement than content-inspection DLP. Reported customers include Micron, Peloton, ClickUp, and Wix.

Bay joins a growing list of AI agent governance vendors, including Hush Security, Straiker, Rein Security, and Geordie. Whether this becomes its own category or folds into DSPM and insider-risk tooling is still unclear.
