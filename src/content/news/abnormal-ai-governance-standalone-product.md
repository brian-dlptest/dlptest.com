**Abnormal AI** made its AI Governance product available on its own, separate from the Abnormal Platform it launched as part of in July 2026. The standalone version lets organizations use Abnormal's behavioral AI to discover and govern the AI used across their business without first deploying Abnormal's inbound email security product.

The company says AI Governance is built to answer two questions boards and executives keep asking: what AI is in use, and what it costs.

Abnormal AI has previously been covered here for its email DLP rules. This product sits closer to shadow-AI discovery than classic DLP, but it follows the same AI-usage-visibility pattern that's shown up in other vendor platforms this year.
