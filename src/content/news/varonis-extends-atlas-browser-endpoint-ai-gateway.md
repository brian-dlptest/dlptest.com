**Varonis** announced three new capabilities for its Atlas platform on November 9, 2026: Browser Protection, Endpoint Protection, and an AI Gateway, all available immediately, according to a company press release.

Endpoint Protection detects AI applications, coding agents, local model files, and MCP servers on computers, extending Atlas's AI Detection & Response down to the device level. The new AI Gateway sits in front of AI traffic, letting teams route requests across model providers, cap token spending, and enforce controls on prompts, responses, and agent tool calls.

Varonis is known for data-classification and activity-monitoring tools built around cloud and SaaS data stores. This move puts that core further out at the edges, into the browser and the endpoint, which is where a lot of shadow AI use actually happens. That puts Varonis in more direct competition with Cyera, Island, and Netskope on agentic-era data controls, not just cloud and SaaS DSPM.
