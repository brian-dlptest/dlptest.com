**HYCU** launched **aiR**, a platform that uses backup data for sensitive data discovery, insider risk detection, identity posture management, and AI agent governance. HYCU's argument is that backup vendors already hold a full copy of enterprise data, so they can answer visibility questions across SaaS applications without adding agents or API integrations.

#### What else is going on

A lot is moving around this launch. DSPM is consolidating, with **Veeam** reportedly close to a $1.8B acquisition of **Securiti** and **Cyera** raising $400M earlier this year at a $9B valuation. Identity posture management is still spread across vendors like **Veza** and **Permiso**, and AI agent governance doesn't have a clear leader yet. HYCU is pitching aiR as one source for all four use cases.

#### How it works

aiR uses backup snapshots to find sensitive data, detect unusual user behavior, surface identity and access posture issues, and monitor AI agent activity. Backup data is already collected, retained, and historical, so HYCU says you need less separate discovery and classification infrastructure.

#### Analysts

Six analysts are on the record for the launch, from IDC, DCIG, Coldago, Data Protection Matters, and Acrew Capital. IDC analysts **Johnny Yu** and **Jennifer Glenn** describe sensitive data discovery, insider risk, identity posture, and agent governance as one platform question and not four separate purchases.

#### Competition

**Enrique Salem**, partner at Bain Capital Ventures and former Symantec CEO, is on the record positioning aiR against dedicated visibility vendors. That puts HYCU up against **Varonis**, **Cyera**, **BigID**, **Code42**, **Veza**, and **Permiso**. HYCU is also going after the 500 to 10,000 employee segment, which has been underserved by enterprise-priced tools.

aiR is available now.
