**Fortinet** announced new capabilities for its unified endpoint platform, FortiEndpoint, aimed at helping organizations adopt AI and keep sensitive data under control.

The part that matters for DLP teams is native data loss prevention for AI interactions, which inspects sensitive data going to AI apps, agents, and external services. It's bundled with AI visibility and governance, endpoint risk and compliance scoring, adaptive zero-trust access, and a FortiAI assistant for natural-language investigations.

So the platform and SSE vendors are pushing GenAI DLP down to the endpoint, and they aren't leaving it to the DSPM crowd and AI DLP startups like MIND, Jazz, and Orion. Inspecting at the endpoint, before data hits a browser tab or an unmanaged AI service, is a different control point than network or API approaches.

Two caveats:

- This is an existing vendor adding controls to an existing agent. That can be good (one agent, one console) or limiting, depending on how deep the detection actually goes.
- Fortinet says this is slated for **Q3 2026 availability**, so it's announced but not generally available. Treat it as roadmap until you can test detection quality and coverage in your own environment, especially managed AI apps versus shadow AI.

If you're already evaluating endpoint DLP or GenAI data controls, add FortiEndpoint to your list and test the claims once it ships.
