MIND™, a data loss prevention (DLP) startup, has emerged from stealth with $11 million in seed funding from YL Ventures and other cybersecurity leaders. MIND is going after the growing complexity and volume of data, especially unstructured data with confidential information. Its AI platform identifies, monitors, and prevents data leaks across SaaS, on-premises, and AI applications, and MIND says it significantly reduces false alerts and the resources needed to run DLP.

The founders are cybersecurity veterans, including Eran Barak (CEO), Itai Schwartz (CTO), and Hod Bin Noon (VP of R&D). The Seattle-based company combines AI, risk assessment, and automation to secure data at rest, in motion, and in use. It has praise from industry leaders like Justin Somaini of YL Ventures and Ryan Fritts of Everon.
