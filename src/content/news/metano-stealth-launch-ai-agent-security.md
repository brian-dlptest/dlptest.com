San Jose startup **Metano** emerged from stealth with an AI agent security and governance platform, backed by $12 million in seed funding led by Sierra Ventures. The platform is meant to help enterprises scale AI adoption without losing track of what agents can access.

Its core module, called Hive, connects agent tasks, identities, tools, data access and actions through a shared security context graph, according to the company. Metano's founders previously worked at ServiceNow, Zscaler, Palo Alto Networks, FireEye and Microsoft.

The data-access angle puts Metano in the same category as other recent stealth launches focused on guarding what AI agents can touch, including Archestra, Ent, Bay and Neo. The field is getting crowded, and differentiation among those platforms is still unclear.
