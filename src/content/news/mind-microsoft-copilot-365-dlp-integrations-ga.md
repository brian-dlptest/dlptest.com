**MIND** announced that its DLP integrations for Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft 365, Purview, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Edge and Exchange are now generally available, through a partnership with **Microsoft**. The integrations are meant to automatically discover, classify, detect and prevent sensitive data loss across those apps.

MIND positions the platform as an additional layer alongside Microsoft Purview's native Copilot protections, combining AI-driven classification with real-time enforcement and automated remediation. The product uses the Microsoft Graph API to discover and classify data, including information in Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive.

MIND has been a repeat name in DLP news this year, including a Series B round and other product launches. This release is a concrete example of a third-party DLP vendor racing to cover the Copilot blast radius instead of leaning only on Purview's native controls.
