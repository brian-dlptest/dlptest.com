**Geordie AI** raised **$30 million in Series A funding** led by **Balderton Capital**, with participation from **Crosspoint Capital** and existing investors **General Catalyst** and **Ten Eleven Ventures**. The company says the round will fund expansion of its security and governance platform for autonomous AI agents.

### Why This Belongs on a Data Security Watchlist

This is not traditional DLP, and Geordie is not pitching itself as a DSPM vendor. The relevance for data-security buyers is narrower but real: autonomous agents create a new class of non-human actors that can access tools, retrieve data, trigger workflows, and move information across systems faster than human-centric controls were designed to inspect.

Geordie describes its platform as giving enterprises a real-time view of which agents exist, what they can access, how they behave, and what risks they create across enterprise systems. Its runtime remediation suite, **Beam**, is positioned around shaping and constraining agent behavior without halting deployment.

### Practitioner Take

For DLP and DSPM teams, agent governance is becoming adjacent to the same questions they already ask about employees, service accounts, and SaaS integrations: what sensitive data can this identity reach, what is normal behavior, and when should the organization intervene?

The buyer risk is category sprawl. If every AI security vendor becomes "governance," security teams will struggle to separate policy orchestration from actual enforcement. Geordie's funding is worth tracking because it focuses on agent access and behavior at runtime, which is closer to operational data protection than another generic AI readiness narrative.
