**Varonis**, one of the longest-standing names in enterprise data security, is reportedly working with advisers and weighing its options, including a potential sale, after preliminary takeover interest from private equity firms Blackstone, Thoma Bravo, and Vista Equity Partners. Bloomberg reported it, and BankInfoSecurity and others relayed it on June 23.

Keep in mind this is an *exploratory* process sourced to unnamed people. No deal, price, or buyer is confirmed, Varonis declined to comment, and PE "interest" often goes nowhere. The stock jumped roughly 11% on the report, but that only tells you what Wall Street thinks.

## Why DLP and DSPM buyers should care

Varonis is on a lot of shortlists for data classification, access governance, insider risk monitoring (UEBA), and more and more for AI and DSPM with its "Atlas" branding. A take-private would affect roadmap, pricing, and channel relationships, which are the things that bite you in a multi-year deployment. If you're mid-evaluation against **Cyera**, **Sentra**, **Microsoft Purview**, or **Proofpoint**, ask your Varonis account team about renewal terms and roadmap commitments before you sign anything long.

## The backdrop

This follows a rough stretch. Varonis is valued around $4.1B, with the stock well below its 2025 highs after an October 2025 disclosure of softening renewals on its on-prem subscription base, the legacy revenue it has been trying to move to cloud-native SaaS. Meanwhile cloud-native rival Cyera reportedly went from a $6B valuation (June 2025) to $12B (June 2026). Wedbush's Dan Ives called Varonis undervalued against peers (~3.9x 2027 EV/revenue against ~7.3x for comparable names), which is part of why financial buyers are interested.

## What to do

Nothing changes today. But if Varonis is your current vendor or a finalist, plan for a change of ownership now. Confirm support SLAs, get roadmap items in writing where you can, and keep a real second option. PE ownership can mean disciplined investment, or it can mean price increases and slower innovation. We don't know yet.
