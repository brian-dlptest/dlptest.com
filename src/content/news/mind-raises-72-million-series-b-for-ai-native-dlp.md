**MIND**, a data loss prevention startup led by the former CEO of Hexadite, closed a $72 million Series B led by **Crosspoint Capital Partners**, with existing investors **YL Ventures** and **Paladin Capital Group** returning. It's the company's second raise in just over a year, following a $30 million Series A, and pushes total disclosed funding past $100 million. MIND says the money goes toward platform development and enterprise go-to-market.

It's in the crowded "AI-era DLP" group that buyers are weighing along with **Cyberhaven** and **Concentric AI**. (Disclaimer: I work at Cyberhaven.) Discover sensitive data across SaaS apps, endpoints, and email, classify it on both content and context, and add blocking or user nudges on top. MIND is trying to stand out by using AI agents to triage which events actually need an analyst and which are just noise. That goes straight at the alert fatigue that has been a problem for content inspection DLP for years.

Most DLP platforms now market some version of content plus context detection, so the classification isn't the news. The news is that investors are still funding next-gen DLP on the promise of AI agent triage. I'd watch how **Microsoft Purview** and **Proofpoint** respond, especially as AI agents themselves become a new way for data to leave.
