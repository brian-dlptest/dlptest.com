**CrowdStrike** is in talks to acquire **Zscaler**, according to two people familiar with the matter, Reuters reported Friday. The talks are ongoing and no deal is guaranteed.

Zscaler has a market capitalization of around $33.5 billion. Its shares jumped 17% in after-hours trading on the news, while CrowdStrike's shares fell 8%.

Zscaler's security service edge stack includes cloud and web DLP along with policy enforcement that many enterprises already route sensitive traffic through. Folding that into CrowdStrike's Falcon endpoint platform would combine endpoint and network data controls under one vendor. Reuters frames the deal as one that could plug CrowdStrike's network-security gap as customers trim their vendor lists.

No definitive agreement has been announced, so the valuation and terms could still change.
