Sentra, a cloud-native data security vendor, raised a $50 million Series B led by Key1 Capital, taking its total funding past $100 million. The company reports over 300% year-over-year growth and has added multiple Fortune 500 customers. The money goes toward its Cloud-Native Data Security Platform (DSP), AI security capabilities, and growing the team.

Sentra covers data visibility and control across multi-cloud, on-premises, and AI environments, and claims over 95% classification accuracy using large language models. It says its approach is low overhead and doesn't disrupt workflows.

It was founded by cybersecurity veterans from Israel's Unit 8200 and was recently named a "Leader" and "Fast Mover" by GigaOm. Sentra will show its expanded platform at RSA Conference 2025.
