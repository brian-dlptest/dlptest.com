**Proofpoint** announced an integration with **Anthropic's** Claude Compliance API that brings supported Claude Enterprise and Claude Platform activity into Proofpoint's data security, insider risk, and digital communications governance workflows.

The important part for DLP teams is not just that Claude gets another enterprise connector. Proofpoint says customers can apply existing classifiers, behavioral risk models, and data loss prevention policies to Claude prompts, responses, files, projects, and administrative activity, while Claude Platform activity logs feed into the same investigation context used for endpoint, email, cloud, and collaboration channels.

### Why This Matters for DLP

AI assistants are becoming another place where sensitive data is read, summarized, rewritten, and moved. If Claude activity sits outside the DLP and insider-risk stack, teams can approve enterprise AI adoption but still lose visibility into the actual content and behavior that create risk.

Proofpoint is positioning the integration as a unified control layer: humans and AI agents should be governed through the same data classification, behavioral analytics, supervision, retention, eDiscovery, and investigation workflows rather than through a separate AI-only console.

### Practitioner Take

For existing Proofpoint customers, this is a practical step toward treating sanctioned AI systems as first-class enterprise data channels. The diligence question is how much Claude content each deployment mode exposes through the Compliance API, and whether policy actions are strong enough for prevention or primarily improve monitoring, investigation, and defensible governance.
