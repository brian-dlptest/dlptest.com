**Proofpoint** announced an integration with Anthropic's Claude Compliance API that brings Claude Enterprise conversation content and Claude Platform activity logs into Proofpoint's data security, insider risk, AI runtime security, and Digital Communications Governance workflows.

For DLP teams, the important point is not that Claude gets another audit export. Proofpoint is positioning the integration as an extension of existing controls: classifiers, behavioral risk models, and data loss prevention policies can be applied to prompts, responses, uploaded files, projects, and administrative activity in Claude Enterprise. Claude Platform support is narrower, focused on activity logs rather than prompt and response content.

### Why This Matters for DLP Buyers

The practical buyer question is whether AI activity becomes a separate console and policy set, or whether it can be governed inside the same workflow already used for endpoint, email, cloud, and collaboration activity. Proofpoint is clearly arguing for the latter.

That matters because many enterprise DLP programs are already struggling with operational load. Adding AI-specific tools for every assistant or agent surface risks creating another disconnected queue. Folding supported Claude activity into existing data security and insider-risk investigations may make it easier to answer familiar questions: what sensitive data was involved, who or what acted on it, what happened before and after, and whether the same activity would have triggered a control outside AI.

### A Narrow but Relevant AI Control Surface

This is still an integration with a specific AI platform, not a universal answer to GenAI leakage. Coverage depends on customers using Anthropic-hosted Claude Enterprise or Claude Platform and on what the Compliance API exposes for each environment.

Still, the announcement is directionally important for practitioners. It moves AI governance closer to day-to-day DLP and insider-risk operations instead of treating AI as a standalone compliance sidecar. For organizations already standardized on Proofpoint, Claude becomes another monitored work surface where sensitive data movement and risky behavior can be evaluated under the same control model.
