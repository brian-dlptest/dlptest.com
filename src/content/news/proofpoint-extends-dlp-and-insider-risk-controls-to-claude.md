**Proofpoint** announced an integration with **Anthropic's Claude Compliance API** that brings Claude Enterprise conversation content and Claude Platform activity logs into Proofpoint's data security, insider risk, and communications governance workflows.

The practical pitch is straightforward: customers can apply the same classifiers, behavioral risk models, DLP policies, supervision rules, retention policies, eDiscovery, and investigation processes they already use for endpoint, email, cloud, and collaboration activity to supported Claude activity.

### Why It Matters for DLP

AI usage creates a familiar DLP problem in a new channel. Employees and agents can place sensitive files, prompts, responses, and project context into a system that may sit outside the evidence trails security teams have spent years building.

Proofpoint is positioning the Claude integration as an extension of existing controls rather than a separate AI security stack. That matters for practitioners because parallel control planes often create policy drift: one set of classifiers for email, another for endpoint, and a third for AI tools.

### What Teams Can Monitor

For Claude Enterprise, Proofpoint says organizations can evaluate prompts, responses, files, projects, and administrative activity. For Claude Platform, the integration monitors activity logs. The goal is to let security teams investigate AI-assisted workflows inside the same risk framework they use for human activity.

This is less about blocking a single chatbot and more about whether DLP vendors can make AI activity first-class telemetry. If AI assistants and agents are now participating in the same workflows as employees, DLP programs need context that spans both rather than treating AI as a disconnected exception.
