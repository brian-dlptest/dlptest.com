**Teleskope** launched Teleskope for Slack, a native integration on the Slack App Marketplace. It discovers, classifies, and remediates sensitive data across Slack messages, files, canvases, and lists, both live and historical, on Slack Enterprise Grid.

The pitch is fixing the problem in place, not another alert console. Slack is where a lot of secrets, PII, and financial data end up, and Teleskope wants to resolve that automatically and not send it to a queue. The company cites a sub-two-second SLA for detection and remediation. It also has a customer proof point from **Ramp**: 100 percent of remediation workflows automated, with roughly one hour of active management per week.

Teleskope calls DSPM its primary category and DLP secondary, and this is a real capability on a high-risk SaaS app. It's still one SaaS integration from a vendor whose last news was a $25M raise, so it's on the smaller side as far as launches go. Does automated remediation hold up against real false positive rates? Test the SLA and the Ramp automation number in a proof of concept before you assume you can set it and forget it.
