**iboss** announced a new AI DLP capability that reads content in real time with AI/ML, without relying on data labels, to detect, flag, and block sensitive data on its way to any destination, from AI platforms to cloud apps.

A second outlet covering the launch said the product cuts dependence on correctly labeled documents and includes data-loss risk scoring plus one-click compliance reports for GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, and SOX. iboss has been iterating quickly on its DLP and CASB stack through 2026, and this launch is separate from its earlier shadow-AI-visibility release.

Label-free, content-aware inspection is being pitched as an alternative to classic EDM and fingerprint DLP across a lot of vendors right now. Independent false-positive and accuracy benchmarks for these claims are harder to find than the announcements.
