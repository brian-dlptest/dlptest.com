**Netskope** used Black Hat USA 2026 to launch Netskope One DataSec Command Center, a control plane it positions as the orchestration layer sitting over all of its data-security enforcement points.

For DLP and DSPM buyers, the pitch is consolidation. The Command Center provides an always-on discovery dashboard and a risk-prioritization engine that normalizes signals from DLP, DSPM, SWG and CASB, aiming to surface exposures across SaaS, IaaS, PaaS, on-prem, inline and endpoint from one place. It ships alongside a "DLP AISecOps Agent" for automated triage — Netskope's answer to alert fatigue. The company cites its own figure that roughly half of organizations investigate half or fewer of the alerts they generate; treat that as a vendor number rather than independent fact.

**Practitioner read:** For teams already standardized on Netskope One (SSE/CASB), this is an incremental platform play, not a rip-and-replace. Strategically it's a direct shot at standalone DSPM dashboards that "find but don't fix" by folding discovery, prioritization and enforcement under one roof.

The correlation-across-every-environment framing is exactly the kind of claim that needs POC validation. Pressure-test on-prem and endpoint coverage depth specifically — those are typically where unified-platform stories thin out compared with inline and SaaS. Confirm how the AISecOps agent handles triage decisions you'd want a human to own before relying on it in production.
