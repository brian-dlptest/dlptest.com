**KnowBe4** announced two additions to **Prevent**, its outbound email security product: a **DLP rule builder** and **misdirected content analysis**. Both are scheduled to become available in June 2026 and are positioned as advanced data protection features for all Prevent customers, with an explicit emphasis on small and mid-market organizations.

### What Changed

The DLP rule builder gives administrators a way to create their own outbound email blocking rules without relying on a separate professional services project. KnowBe4's example is a rule that blocks only when a message contains multiple types of sensitive data, which is the kind of condition teams often use to reduce false positives and user friction.

The misdirected content analysis feature adds a second decision point: not just what is in the message, but whether the recipient context looks wrong. KnowBe4 says the AI-powered warning appears before a user sends the email, turning likely mistakes into a chance to correct behavior before data leaves the organization.

### Why DLP Buyers Should Notice

Email DLP remains one of the most practical data-loss channels to evaluate because accidental sharing is easy to reproduce and measure. This release is not a full enterprise DLP platform replacement, but it does point to a market pattern: outbound email controls are becoming more self-service, context-aware, and packaged for buyers who cannot afford months of tuning before value appears.

For practitioners, the interesting detail is the combination of **content-aware DLP** and **recipient-aware warning logic** in one standard outbound workflow. That is closer to how real leakage happens: sensitive content, sent to the wrong person, by a user who often did not intend to create an incident.
