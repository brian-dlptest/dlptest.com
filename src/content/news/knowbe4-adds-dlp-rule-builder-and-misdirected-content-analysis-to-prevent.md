**KnowBe4** announced two additions to **Prevent**, its outbound email security product: a DLP rule builder and AI-powered misdirected content analysis. The company says the features will be available in June 2026 and are aimed especially at small and mid-market organizations that need data protection controls without a large DLP operations team.

### What Changed

The **Prevent DLP Rule Builder** lets administrators create rules for outbound email, including conditions that only block or warn when multiple pieces of sensitive data appear together. That matters because SMB-focused DLP often fails in one of two ways: it is too coarse to be trusted, or it demands the same policy-authoring effort as enterprise suites.

The **Misdirected Content Analysis** feature adds a recipient-aware check before a message leaves the organization. Rather than inspecting only the message body or attachments, it looks for situations where sensitive content appears headed to the wrong person and warns the sender before the mistake becomes an incident.

### DLPTest Take

This is not a broad DSPM platform move, but it is a practical DLP story: outbound email remains one of the most common paths for accidental data loss. KnowBe4 is framing the launch around human error rather than malicious insiders, which fits the market shift toward inline coaching and lower-friction controls.

For buyers, the useful question is how much tuning the rule builder actually removes. If Prevent can pair content inspection with relationship-aware recipient analysis without creating noisy holds, it gives smaller security teams a more realistic path than standing up a heavyweight DLP program from scratch.
