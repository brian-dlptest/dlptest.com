**KnowBe4** announced two additions to **Prevent**, its outbound email security product: a self-service **DLP rule builder** and AI-powered **misdirected content analysis**. The company says both features will be available in June 2026 and will be included for all customers.

### Why this is a DLP story

The DLP rule builder is the more traditional control. Administrators can create rules for outbound email, including conditions that only block when multiple sensitive-data indicators are present. That matters for smaller teams because legacy email DLP often fails on the same two problems: too much tuning work and too many false positives.

The misdirected content analysis is the behavior layer. Instead of asking only whether an email contains sensitive data, Prevent also evaluates whether the recipient looks wrong for the content and warns the user before send. For organizations where most outbound leaks are mistakes rather than malicious insider events, that combination is closer to practical email DLP than another alert queue.

### DLPTest take

This is not a broad enterprise DLP platform announcement. It is narrower and likely aimed at KnowBe4's SMB and mid-market base. But the direction is worth watching: email DLP controls are being packaged as workflow correction, not just policy enforcement. That framing could resonate with buyers who need PII and financial-record protection but do not have a full-time DLP operations function.
