**KnowBe4** announced two new features for **KnowBe4 Prevent**, its outbound email security product: a **DLP rule builder** for admin-defined blocking policies, and **misdirected content analysis** that uses AI to warn users before sensitive data goes to the wrong recipient.

It's aimed at small and mid-market organizations that want more control over outbound data without running a big DLP program. The features are scheduled for June 2026.

### Why it matters for email DLP

Outbound email is still one of the hardest channels because the risky action often looks like normal work: a spreadsheet attached to the wrong thread, PII sent to a similar-looking contact, or a message that should have stayed internal. This release goes after that human error.

The rule builder is the more traditional control. Admins can define policies that block unauthorized messages, including rules that only trigger when multiple sensitive data conditions appear together. That kind of tuning matters because false positives are one of the main reasons email DLP programs lose business support.

### The interesting part

What stands out is pairing content inspection with recipient analysis. A policy engine can tell what's in a message. Misdirected content analysis asks if the recipient makes sense. If you're evaluating email DLP or insider risk tools, test that combination against real near misses, especially if accidents cause more of your incidents than malicious exfiltration.
