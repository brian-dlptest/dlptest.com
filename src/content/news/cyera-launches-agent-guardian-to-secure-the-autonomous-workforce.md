**Cyera** launched Agent Guardian at Black Hat USA 2026 to discover, monitor and govern AI agents — guarding against prompt injection and unauthorized data access — alongside Cyera Endpoint, which extends those guardrails to local and desktop AI tools running on employee machines.

The endpoint piece is what makes this relevant to data-protection teams rather than pure agent-governance noise. Cyera Endpoint acts as a data-egress control at the device, adjacent to endpoint DLP, layered on Cyera's existing DSPM and Omni DLP stack. For buyers already running Cyera classification, it bolts agent visibility and runtime enforcement onto data they've already inventoried and labeled.

Cyera frames the problem as an explosion of non-human identities inside the enterprise — it cites a 480% jump in NHIs at Fortune 500 companies over six months, and notes agents inheriting elevated human permissions the moment they're deployed. Those are the vendor's own figures and should be attributed as such.

**Practitioner read:** Cyera was one of many vendors — Rubrik, SailPoint, Sweet Security, Zero Networks and others — shipping near-identical MCP/agent-security products the same week, so differentiation is unproven. Treat this as directional, not a category-defining moment. The genuinely evaluable part for data teams is Cyera Endpoint: ask how its egress controls compare with a dedicated endpoint DLP agent, and whether it duplicates or displaces tooling you already run on the device.
