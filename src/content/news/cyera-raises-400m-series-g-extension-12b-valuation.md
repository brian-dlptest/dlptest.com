**Cyera** has closed a $400 million Series G extension, pushing its valuation past $12 billion. The round is backed by **Goldman Sachs**, and it lands just months after the company's valuation climbed from $6B to $9B to $12B earlier in 2026 across a rapid sequence of prior raises.

For buyers evaluating the DSPM and broader data security market, the headline number matters less than what it signals about pace. Cyera is now raising capital at a clip few, if any, rivals in the data security posture management space can match — this is at least the third distinct "Cyera raises at ~$12B" cycle reported this year. That kind of near-continuous fundraising gives the company sustained firepower for the bolt-on acquisitions it has been using to widen its platform, including recent pickups like **Trail Security** and **Genie Security**, which extended Cyera's footprint into browser controls, data lineage, and governance for MCP/agent-based AI workflows.

The practical implication for security and data protection teams: Cyera is positioning itself as a consolidation platform across DSPM, DLP-adjacent controls, and emerging AI-agent governance, rather than a point-solution vendor. Organizations already invested in Cyera's platform should expect continued feature expansion via acquisition, while competitors will need to match both product breadth and the balance-sheet endurance this level of funding buys. As with any hot-market valuation run, though, the multiple itself says more about investor appetite for AI-security consolidation plays than it does about immediate customer-facing differentiation — worth watching whether the M&A pace translates into integrated, coherent tooling rather than a loosely stitched portfolio.
