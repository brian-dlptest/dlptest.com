**Cyera** closed a $400 million Series G extension, pushing its valuation past $12 billion. **Goldman Sachs** backed the round, which comes just months after the company's valuation went from $6B to $9B to $12B earlier in 2026.

Cyera is raising money faster than any of its DSPM rivals can match. This is at least the third "Cyera raises at ~$12B" cycle reported this year. All that money pays for the acquisitions it has been using to build out its platform, including **Trail Security** and **Genie Security**, which extended Cyera into browser controls, data lineage, and governance for MCP and agent-based AI workflows.

So Cyera wants to be one platform across DSPM, DLP, and AI agent governance. If you're already on Cyera, expect more features to show up through acquisitions. The valuation says more about how much investors like AI security consolidation than about what customers get today. What I'd watch is if all these acquisitions turn into a product that works together.
