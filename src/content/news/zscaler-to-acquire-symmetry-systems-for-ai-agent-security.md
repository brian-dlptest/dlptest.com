**Zscaler** (NASDAQ: ZS) announced its intent to acquire **Symmetry Systems**, a startup focused on identity mapping and data access graphs for AI security. The deal is expected to close in the coming days.

Symmetry builds an **access graph**. It takes in access logs from SaaS applications, public cloud services, data stores, and AI systems, then uses AI to map which identities, human and non-human, are accessing which data and how. Zscaler plans to add this to its **Zero Trust Exchange** so security teams can build and enforce policies for how AI agents communicate with applications, data, and each other.

### The AI agent governance problem

Identity governance has always relied on user directories that put people into stable groups with mapped permissions. AI agents break that. They work autonomously across systems with short-lived identities and inherited permissions, and their numbers are growing exponentially. Policies built for people don't scale to millions of agents.

Zscaler CEO **Jay Chaudhry** said the access graph gives the Zero Trust Exchange the visibility it needs to govern agent-to-application and agent-to-agent communication at scale.

### What Zscaler highlighted

- **Least-privilege policies for AI:** map granted versus actual-use permissions across every identity, then enforce minimum necessary access through Zero Trust Exchange
- **Full data lineage:** trace any data an AI agent touches through chains of sub-agents and tools for audit and compliance
- **Real-time anomaly detection:** surface unexpected agent behavior and trigger automated Zero Trust Exchange responses
- **Blast radius calculation:** see what data and systems are at risk if an agent or identity is compromised

### Why it matters

SSE and zero trust vendors keep pulling DSPM capabilities into their platforms. Symmetry's access graph sits between DSPM and identity security, and that visibility matters more as AI agents spread. The biggest security vendors are now putting real money into agent governance.

### Competition

Zscaler already had AI discovery and runtime guardrails, and Symmetry fills in the identity-to-data mapping. It puts Zscaler up against other platforms working on agentic AI security, including Palo Alto Networks (recent Protect AI acquisition), CrowdStrike (AI model scanning), and the DSPM vendors. With Proofpoint acquiring Normalyze and Veeam reportedly pursuing Securiti AI, this is one more data security startup getting bought by a bigger platform.
