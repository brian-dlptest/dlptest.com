**Corrata** announced new **AI governance** and enhanced **mobile data loss prevention** capabilities for iOS and Android environments, positioning mobile devices as a DLP blind spot rather than only a mobile-threat-defense problem.

The company says its AI governance controls let organizations define sanctioned AI services and SDKs for the mobile workspace, then flag or block unsanctioned services including consumer chatbots, coding assistants, and embedded LLM SDKs in third-party apps. Its DLP update uses traffic analysis to detect sensitive corporate data being sent to external services.

### Mobile as an AI exfiltration path

The useful practitioner angle is not that mobile devices need another acronym. It is that shadow AI behavior is increasingly happening through phones and tablets where personal and work contexts are already blurred. If an employee uploads a customer file to a consumer AI service from a mobile app, many legacy DLP programs will have less telemetry than they do on managed Windows or macOS endpoints.

Corrata's release does not remove the usual evaluation questions around classifier accuracy, policy granularity, user experience, and incident workflow integration. But it is a reminder that endpoint DLP coverage discussions should include mobile AI usage, not just laptops, email, SaaS, and browsers.
