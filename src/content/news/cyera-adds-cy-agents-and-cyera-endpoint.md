**Cyera** unveiled two new products at its datasecAI 2026 event on November 10, 2026: Cy Agents and Cyera Endpoint. Both extend Cyera's Omni platform.

Cy Agents is a set of specialized AI sub-agents, named Detect, Triage, and Learn, layered onto Cyera's existing "Cy" assistant. Cyera Endpoint is the more concrete DLP piece: an on-device sensor that brings AI security and data loss prevention to corporate laptops and workstations.

Cyera has built its DSPM position largely through acquisition, picking up Trail, Oasis, Ryft, and Genie Security. Cyera Endpoint marks a shift toward native, in-house endpoint DLP rather than acquired capability.
