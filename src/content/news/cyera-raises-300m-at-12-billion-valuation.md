**Cyera** has reportedly raised **$300 million** at a **$12 billion valuation**, according to **Calcalist**, with **Evolution Equity Partners** leading and investors including **Georgian**, **Greenoaks**, **Lightspeed Venture Partners**, **Sequoia Capital**, **Sapphire Ventures**, **Redpoint Ventures**, **Cyberstarts**, **Coatue**, **Accel**, and **Spark Capital** participating.

The timing is the headline: Cyera raised $400 million at a $9 billion valuation in January, so this round extends a rapid valuation climb rather than resetting the story. Calcalist says the new financing brings total capital raised to more than $2 billion.

### Why it matters for DLP and DSPM

Cyera is now trying to make the case that **Data Security Posture Management**, **Data Loss Prevention**, AI security, identity context, and acquired capabilities such as endpoint protection belong in one data-security platform. That is a very different buyer conversation from the original DSPM pitch of finding sensitive data and risky access in cloud stores.

For DLP buyers, the signal is that the market is rewarding vendors that can connect discovery, classification, access governance, and control enforcement. The practical diligence question remains whether those pieces behave like one workflow in production or like several acquired products sharing a roadmap.
