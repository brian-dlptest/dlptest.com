**Cyberhaven** introduced Agentic Data Security (ADS) on November 5, 2026, a new module in its Cyberhaven Flow platform. The module extends Cyberhaven's existing data-lineage approach from human users to AI agents, giving security teams visibility and control over what agents do with corporate data.

Cyberhaven Flow already combines data lineage, AI, and autonomous protection to deliver DLP, insider risk management, and DSPM for human users. ADS applies those same policies and controls to data accessed by AI agents.

The module follows Cyberhaven's earlier extension covering ChatGPT Enterprise and Claude, but ADS is broader and agent-agnostic. Point solutions like Bay, Reco, and Sentra are also building controls for agentic AI. Who gets there first with enterprise buyers is still an open question.
