Teleskope, an "agentic" data security company, raised **$25 million in Series A funding** led by **M13**, with **Primary Venture Partners** and **Lerer Hippeau** continuing to back it. That brings total funding to **$32.2 million**.

The money goes toward product, expansion, and go-to-market for its **agentic data security platform**, which Teleskope calls the first of its kind designed to work like a human security team at scale.

The platform **discovers, classifies, and protects sensitive data** across cloud, SaaS, and hybrid environments. It uses a **multi-stage AI pipeline** to understand context, apply company-specific policies, and automatically **detect, prioritize, and remediate risk** in real time.

Founder and CEO **Elizabeth Nammour**, a former Airbnb security engineer, started Teleskope after running into the limits of legacy tools firsthand.

"Teleskope gives enterprises a fully agentic platform that can automatically detect, contextualize, and remediate risk," said Nammour. "It's like having a 24/7 embedded security team."

Customers include **Ramp, LegalZoom, Alloy, GoFundMe**, and **The Atlantic**. **Karl Alomar**, Managing Partner at M13 and former COO of DigitalOcean, has joined its board.
