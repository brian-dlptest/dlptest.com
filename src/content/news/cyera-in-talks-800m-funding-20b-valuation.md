**Cyera**, the DSPM vendor that closed a $1 billion round at a $12 billion valuation in June, is reportedly back in the market. Calcalist first reported on October 30 that Cyera held preliminary discussions with investors about raising capital at a $20 billion valuation, citing a report by The Information.

By November 6, Gurufocus reported the talks had advanced: Cyera is in advanced discussions for a new round aiming to raise approximately $800 million, which could put its valuation at $20 billion, up from $12 billion in June.

This is still talks, not a closed round. Terms, lead investor, and final size could all change before anything is signed. If it closes, it would be Cyera's fourth headline raise in roughly 18 months, on top of a run of acquisitions including Trail, reported talks with Oasis, and Genie Security.
