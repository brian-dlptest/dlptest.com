**Zip Security** launched a **Data Loss Prevention** capability for Google Workspace focused on finding and remediating publicly exposed Google Drive files. The product is available now and is aimed at teams that need exposure reduction without running a dedicated DLP program.

Zip says the feature connects directly to Google Workspace, analyzes file names, permissions, metadata, and up to 180 days of sharing history, then surfaces exposed files with a plain-language risk assessment. Admins can revoke public access from the Zip interface, and the company says file contents are not read or stored.

### What this means for DLP programs

This is a narrow but useful slice of DLP: oversharing detection and one-click remediation for Drive. It does not sound like a replacement for content-aware endpoint, email, or SaaS DLP, but it addresses a common leakage path that often lives between IT administration and security operations.

For practitioners, the interesting question is whether metadata-only analysis catches enough risky exposure to matter. If it does, this kind of lightweight SaaS DLP could be attractive for lean teams that want fast cleanup before investing in broader data classification and policy enforcement.
