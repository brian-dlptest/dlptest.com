**Menlo Security** announced the Menlo IRIS Browser Extension, which gives security teams visibility into and control over what happens inside the browsers employees already use, from shadow AI use to risky third-party extensions, according to a company announcement published November 11-12, 2026.

The pitch is speed and low friction: the extension installs in hours with no browser replacement or retooling, giving security teams of any size visibility and control over GenAI use, data movement, files, and third-party extensions.

Menlo Security is known for its secure, isolation-based enterprise browser platform. IRIS is a bet that a lightweight extension can deliver data-movement visibility and GenAI governance without asking employees to switch browsers, which is a lower-friction alternative to full enterprise browser replacements like Island or LayerX. Worth watching whether buyers evaluating the browser as a DLP channel pick the extension route over a full browser swap.
